Many foods that are safe for people are toxic to dogs, and owners should know the common ones. Chocolate, xylitol (a sweetener found in sugar-free gum and some foods), grapes and raisins, onions, garlic, chives, macadamia nuts, alcohol, caffeine, and large amounts of fatty or salty food can all cause illness ranging from stomach upset to organ failure or death. Xylitol and grapes or raisins are especially dangerous even in small amounts. If a dog eats something toxic, contact a vet or an animal poison line immediately and do not wait for symptoms; describe what and how much was eaten. Keeping such foods well out of reach is the best prevention.
