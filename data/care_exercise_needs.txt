Exercise needs vary enormously by breed, age, and health, and matching them is essential to a dog's wellbeing. Working and herding breeds may need one to two hours or more of vigorous activity plus mental work daily, while brachycephalic and companion breeds need much less and can be harmed by overexertion. Beyond physical exercise, mental stimulation through training, sniffing, and puzzle feeders is crucial and often underestimated; a mentally tired dog is calmer than a merely physically tired one. Under-exercised dogs commonly develop destructive or anxious behaviours. Exercise should be adjusted for heat, cold, and a dog's age.
