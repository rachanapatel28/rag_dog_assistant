Dogs thrive on routine because predictability reduces anxiety and supports training. Consistent times for meals, walks, play, toilet breaks, and sleep help a dog understand what to expect and settle more easily. A routine also makes house training faster, since regular feeding leads to regular toileting, and helps prevent boredom-related problems by ensuring daily physical and mental exercise. Everyone in the household should follow the same rules and cues so the dog is not confused by mixed messages. Routines can be flexible over time, but especially for new or anxious dogs, consistency in the early stages is calming and reassuring.
