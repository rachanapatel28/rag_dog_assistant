The Great Dane is a giant breed, gentle and affectionate despite its imposing size, often called a "gentle giant." Their calm temperament makes them surprisingly manageable, but their sheer size means everything from food to vet care is expensive, and they need space and soft bedding to protect their joints. Exercise should be moderate and controlled, especially while young, to protect developing bones. Great Danes are highly prone to bloat (gastric torsion), a life-threatening emergency, and to heart disease, and like most giant breeds they have a short average lifespan of around seven to ten years.
