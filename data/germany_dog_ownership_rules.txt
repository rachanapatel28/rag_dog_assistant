Owning a dog in Germany involves several legal and administrative responsibilities, though specifics vary by federal state (Bundesland) and municipality, so current local rules should always be verified. Most municipalities levy a dog tax (Hundesteuer) that owners must register and pay annually, with rates set locally and often higher for a second dog. Many regions require or strongly recommend dog liability insurance (Hundehaftpflichtversicherung), and in some states it is mandatory. Dogs are commonly required to be registered and identified with a microchip. Leash laws, and rules on which areas dogs may access, are set locally. Certain breeds classified as potentially dangerous face additional requirements in some states.
