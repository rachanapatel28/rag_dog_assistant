The Doberman Pinscher is a large, sleek working breed known for intelligence, loyalty, and alertness. They are highly trainable and bond closely with their families, but their drive and guarding instincts make them better suited to experienced owners who can provide structure, socialisation, and daily exercise. Their short coat offers little insulation, so they feel the cold and need protection in winter. The breed is notably prone to dilated cardiomyopathy, a serious heart condition, as well as a bleeding disorder called von Willebrand disease, so health-tested lines are important.
