The Shiba Inu is a small-to-medium Japanese breed, alert, confident, and famously independent, often described as cat-like. They are clean and relatively quiet but strong-willed, with a high prey drive and unreliable recall, making them a challenging choice for first-time owners. Their thick double coat handles cold well and sheds heavily in seasonal "blows." Shibas can be aloof with strangers and possessive, so early socialisation matters. The breed is generally healthy but can be prone to allergies and luxating patellas.
