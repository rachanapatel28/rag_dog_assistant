The Boxer is a medium-to-large working breed that is playful, energetic, and deeply loyal, often remaining puppy-like for years. They are good with families but need firm, patient training and plenty of exercise to channel their exuberance. As a moderately brachycephalic breed they have reduced heat tolerance and should not be over-exercised in hot weather. Boxers are prone to certain heart conditions and have a higher-than-average cancer risk, giving them a shorter average lifespan, so regular veterinary screening is advisable.
