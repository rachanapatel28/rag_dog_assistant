The Border Collie is a medium herding dog widely regarded as the most intelligent of all breeds. That intelligence comes with an intense need for mental and physical work; a bored Border Collie will invent its own often destructive jobs, so they are not suited to sedentary homes or inexperienced owners. They thrive on training, agility, and herding-style activities. Their double coat handles cold and wet conditions well. The breed is generally healthy and long-lived but can carry inherited eye conditions and a sensitivity to certain medications, which a breeder should test for.
