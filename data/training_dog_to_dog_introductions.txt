Introducing dogs to one another carefully reduces the risk of conflict and helps build positive relationships. Where possible, introduce on neutral territory rather than one dog's home or garden, keep both on loose leashes, and allow a natural, curved approach rather than a tense head-on meeting. Watch body language for relaxed, loose movement versus stiffness, hard stares, or raised hackles, and calmly separate if tension rises. Keep first meetings short and positive, and go for a parallel walk before close interaction. Not every dog wants canine friends, and that is fine; forcing interactions between incompatible dogs tends to backfire.
