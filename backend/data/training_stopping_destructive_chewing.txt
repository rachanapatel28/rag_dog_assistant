Chewing is a normal, necessary behaviour for dogs, especially puppies who explore with their mouths and teethe, so the goal is to redirect it rather than eliminate it. Provide a variety of appropriate chew toys and reward the dog for using them, while managing the environment to keep valuables and hazards out of reach. Destructive chewing in adult dogs is often driven by boredom, anxiety, or excess energy, so increasing exercise and enrichment usually helps. Puppy-proofing and supervision prevent bad habits forming. Punishing chewing after the fact does not work, since the dog cannot connect the punishment to the earlier act; prevention and redirection do.
