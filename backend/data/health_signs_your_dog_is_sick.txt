Recognising early signs of illness helps owners seek care before problems worsen. Warning signs include loss of appetite, lethargy, vomiting or diarrhoea lasting more than a day, difficulty breathing, sudden behaviour changes, limping, excessive drinking or urination, and any collapse. Some situations are emergencies requiring immediate veterinary attention, such as a bloated hard abdomen with unproductive retching, difficulty breathing, suspected poisoning, or inability to urinate. Because dogs instinctively hide weakness, subtle changes in routine, energy, or appetite can be meaningful. When in doubt, contacting a vet is always the safe choice. This information is general and does not replace professional veterinary diagnosis.
