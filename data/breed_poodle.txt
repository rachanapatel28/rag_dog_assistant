The Poodle comes in Standard, Miniature, and Toy sizes and is one of the most intelligent and trainable breeds. Poodles are active, people-oriented, and excel at obedience and dog sports, so they need regular mental challenges as well as physical exercise. Their curly coat sheds very little and is often described as more allergy-friendly, but it requires regular professional grooming to prevent painful matting. They adapt to many climates but the smaller varieties feel the cold. Standard Poodles can be prone to hip dysplasia and bloat, while smaller Poodles are more prone to dental and knee problems.
