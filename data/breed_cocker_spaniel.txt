The Cocker Spaniel is a medium gundog, gentle, affectionate, and eager to please, making it a good family companion. They are moderately energetic and enjoy walks, play, and retrieving. Their long, silky ears and coat require regular grooming and predispose them to ear infections, so ear care is a routine necessity. Cockers can be sensitive dogs that respond best to gentle training. The breed is prone to several eye conditions and ear problems, and some lines to certain temperament issues, so choosing a reputable breeder matters.
