Owning a dog in Germany comes with administrative duties that vary by municipality, so local details should always be confirmed. Most cities require owners to register their dog and pay an annual dog tax (Hundesteuer), with the amount set locally and often higher for additional dogs or for breeds classified as dangerous. Dog liability insurance (Hundehaftpflichtversicherung), which covers damage or injury the dog causes, is mandatory in some federal states and strongly recommended everywhere. Microchipping and registration in a pet database are commonly required or advised. Because rules and rates differ between states and cities, new owners should check requirements directly with their local authority (Bürgeramt) and, in Bavaria, the relevant regional rules.
