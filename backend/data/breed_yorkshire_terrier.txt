The Yorkshire Terrier is a tiny toy breed with a bold, confident terrier personality. Yorkies are affectionate and portable, suiting apartment life, but they can be vocal and need consistent training to curb stubbornness. Their fine, hair-like coat sheds little but requires regular grooming to prevent tangles. Being very small, they are fragile and sensitive to cold. Common health concerns include dental disease, luxating patellas, a windpipe weakness called collapsing trachea, and low blood sugar in puppies, so gentle handling and dental care are priorities.
