The Beagle is a small-to-medium scent hound bred to hunt in packs, which shapes almost everything about the breed. They are friendly, curious, and good with children, but their powerful nose and independent streak make recall training difficult, and they will follow a scent over a command. Beagles are energetic and vocal, prone to howling and baying, which can be an issue in apartments. They tolerate moderate climates but should not be over-exercised in heat. The breed is prone to obesity, ear infections due to their long ears, and certain inherited conditions, so diet and ear care matter.
