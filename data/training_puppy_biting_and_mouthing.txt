Puppy biting and mouthing are completely normal parts of play and exploration and are not signs of aggression, but they need gentle redirection so they do not persist. Puppies learn bite inhibition partly from litter-mates, and owners continue this by reacting to hard bites with a calm pause in play, teaching that biting ends the fun. Redirect the puppy onto appropriate chew toys, and avoid games that encourage biting hands. Ensuring the puppy has enough rest matters too, since overtired puppies often bite more. With consistency, most puppies grow out of mouthing as they mature and their adult teeth come in.
