Siberian Huskies are medium-sized working dogs originally bred in cold Arctic climates.
They have a thick double coat that insulates them against freezing temperatures, which also makes them poorly suited to hot climates like Spain or southern Italy.
Huskies are highly energetic and need substantial daily exercise, making them a challenging choice for first-time owners.
They are known for being friendly, social, and often vocal, but can be independent and prone to escaping if bored.
