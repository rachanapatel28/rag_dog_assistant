Basic obedience training builds communication and safety, and modern training relies on positive reinforcement, rewarding wanted behaviour rather than punishing mistakes. Core cues include sit, stay, come, down, and loose-lead walking. Sessions should be short, frequent, and upbeat, ending on success, because dogs learn better in brief bursts than long drills. Consistency across everyone in the household matters, since mixed signals slow progress. Reliable recall (coming when called) is the most safety-critical skill and is worth heavy investment. Group classes also help with socialisation and give owners professional guidance.
