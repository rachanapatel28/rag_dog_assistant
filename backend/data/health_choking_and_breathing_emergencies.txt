Choking and breathing difficulties are emergencies requiring immediate action and veterinary help. Signs of choking include frantic pawing at the mouth, gagging, distress, and difficulty breathing. If an object is clearly visible and easily reachable it may be possible to remove it gently, but reaching blindly into the throat can push it deeper or cause bites, so caution is vital. Any dog with ongoing breathing difficulty, blue-tinged gums, or collapse needs an emergency vet immediately. Learning basic canine first aid, ideally through a hands-on course, prepares owners to respond calmly. This is general guidance and not a replacement for professional veterinary or emergency care.
