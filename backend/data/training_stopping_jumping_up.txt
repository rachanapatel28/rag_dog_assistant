Dogs often jump up to greet people and seek attention, and the behaviour is unintentionally reinforced when jumping earns attention, even scolding. The most effective fix is to remove the reward: turn away and ignore the dog when it jumps, and give attention only when it has four paws on the floor or is sitting. Teaching and rewarding an alternative greeting, such as sitting, gives the dog a clear way to earn what it wants. Consistency across everyone the dog meets is essential, since one person allowing jumping undermines the training. Managing greetings with a leash or barrier while training helps prevent rehearsal of the habit.
