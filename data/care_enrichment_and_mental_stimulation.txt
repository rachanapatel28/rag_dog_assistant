Mental stimulation is as important as physical exercise, and a lack of it is a common cause of boredom-driven problem behaviours. Enrichment engages a dog's natural instincts to sniff, chew, forage, and problem-solve. Simple options include puzzle feeders, snuffle mats, scatter-feeding in the garden, food-stuffed toys, short training games, and rotating toys to keep them novel. Allowing a dog to sniff freely on walks is powerful enrichment often overlooked in favour of distance. A mentally satisfied dog is generally calmer and more content, and enrichment is especially valuable for high-intelligence breeds and for dogs that must spend some time alone.
