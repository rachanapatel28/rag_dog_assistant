The French Bulldog is a small companion breed with a distinctive flat face and bat-like ears. Frenchies are affectionate, adaptable, and relatively low-energy, which makes them popular for apartment living and first-time owners. However, they are a brachycephalic (short-nosed) breed, so they struggle to breathe efficiently and are highly intolerant of heat and strenuous exercise; hot weather can be genuinely dangerous for them. They also cannot swim well and can drown easily. Common health issues include breathing difficulties, spinal problems, and skin-fold infections, so they often require more veterinary care than their size suggests.
