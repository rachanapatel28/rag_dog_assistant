The Pembroke Welsh Corgi is a small herding breed with short legs and a big personality, intelligent, active, and affectionate. Despite their size they were bred to herd cattle, so they are energetic and need regular exercise and mental work. Their long back and short legs make them prone to spinal problems, so jumping should be limited and weight kept in check. Corgis have a weather-resistant double coat that sheds heavily. They can be vocal and are prone to obesity, which worsens their back and joint risks.
