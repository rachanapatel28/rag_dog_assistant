House training relies on routine, supervision, and consistent positive reinforcement rather than punishment. Take the dog outside frequently, especially after waking, eating, drinking, and play, and reward elimination outdoors immediately so the dog links the location with reward. Puppies have limited bladder control and generally need to go out roughly every couple of hours, so accidents are normal and should be cleaned thoroughly with an enzymatic cleaner to remove scent markers. Punishing accidents tends to make dogs hide rather than learn. Most puppies take several weeks to become reliably house-trained, and consistency shortens the process.
