The English Bulldog is a medium, stocky companion dog with a calm, friendly, and famously stubborn temperament. Their low energy makes them manageable for many owners, but as a brachycephalic breed they have serious heat intolerance and breathing limitations, and they should never be exercised hard or left in warm conditions. Bulldogs are prone to a long list of health problems including breathing disorders, joint issues, and skin infections, and many require caesarean births. Prospective owners should budget for higher-than-average veterinary costs and choose breeders who prioritise health over exaggerated features.
