Travelling safely with a dog protects both the dog and the people in the vehicle. Loose dogs are a hazard in a crash and a distraction while driving, so dogs should be secured, using a crash-tested crate, a secured carrier, or a properly fitted seatbelt harness. Never let a dog ride with its head out of the window, and never leave a dog in a parked car, as interior temperatures rise to lethal levels within minutes even on mild days. Build up to longer journeys gradually for dogs prone to travel sickness or anxiety, and stop for water and toilet breaks on longer trips.
