Introductions to family members and existing pets should be gradual and supervised to set relationships up well. Let a new dog approach people in its own time rather than being crowded, and teach children to be calm and gentle and to respect the dog's space. Introduce dogs to each other on neutral ground where possible, on loose leashes, watching body language for signs of tension, and keep first meetings short and positive. Cats should be introduced slowly, with the cat able to retreat and escape routes available, over days or weeks. Feeding pets separately at first and never forcing interactions reduces conflict.
