The Samoyed is a medium-sized Arctic spitz breed bred to herd reindeer and pull sleds, known for its friendly "smiling" expression and sociable nature. Their thick white double coat provides excellent cold insulation but makes them poorly suited to hot climates and requires frequent grooming to prevent matting. Samoyeds are energetic, need substantial exercise, and are notably vocal and people-oriented, disliking isolation. The breed can be prone to hip dysplasia and a hereditary kidney condition, so health testing is advisable.
