Basic first aid can stabilise a dog and buy time, but it never replaces veterinary care; the priority in any serious situation is to contact a vet or emergency clinic immediately. Knowing your nearest emergency vet and their number in advance saves critical time. A simple first-aid kit might include clean bandages, gauze, tape, tweezers, and your vet's contact details. Even a gentle, injured dog in pain may bite, so handle carefully and consider a makeshift muzzle only if the dog is not vomiting or struggling to breathe. This information is general guidance and is not a substitute for professional veterinary advice.
