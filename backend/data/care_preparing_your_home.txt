Preparing your home before a dog arrives makes the transition safer and calmer, much like childproofing. Secure or remove hazards a curious dog could reach: electrical cords, cleaning products, medications, small swallowable objects, and toxic houseplants. Decide in advance where the dog will sleep, eat, and spend time, and set up a comfortable, quiet resting space. Use baby gates to limit access while the dog learns the rules, and check that the garden or balcony is escape-proof. Having the space ready reduces stress for both dog and owner in those important first days.
