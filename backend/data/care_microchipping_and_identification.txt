Proper identification dramatically improves the chances of a lost dog being reunited with its owner. A microchip is a tiny permanent implant, placed under the skin by a vet, that carries a number linked to the owner's contact details in a registration database; keeping those details up to date is essential for it to work. Microchipping is legally required in many places. In addition to a chip, a collar with a visible ID tag showing a phone number helps a finder make contact quickly. Together, a registered microchip and a clear ID tag are the most reliable way to protect against permanently losing a dog.
