Dental disease is one of the most common health problems in dogs and is largely preventable, yet frequently overlooked. Plaque and tartar build-up lead to gum disease, pain, tooth loss, and bacteria that can affect the heart and kidneys. The gold standard is regular tooth brushing with dog-specific toothpaste, ideally daily, introduced gradually so the dog accepts it. Dental chews, appropriate diets, and professional cleanings under veterinary supervision also help. Small breeds with crowded teeth, such as Yorkies and Chihuahuas, are especially prone and need extra attention. Bad breath is often the first sign of a developing problem.
