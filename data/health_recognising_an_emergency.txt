Some situations are true emergencies where every minute counts and a dog should be taken to a vet immediately. These include difficulty breathing, collapse or unconsciousness, a bloated hard abdomen with unproductive retching, seizures, suspected poisoning, severe bleeding that will not stop, inability to urinate, repeated vomiting, bloating, pale gums, suspected heatstroke, or major trauma such as being hit by a car. Signs like sudden severe pain, extreme lethargy, or a distended abdomen should never be "watched overnight." When unsure whether something is an emergency, calling a vet or emergency clinic for advice is always the safe choice. This is general information, not a diagnosis.
