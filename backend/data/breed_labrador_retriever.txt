The Labrador Retriever is a medium-to-large working and companion dog originally bred in Newfoundland to help fishermen. Labs are friendly, patient, and highly trainable, which makes them one of the best breeds for first-time owners and families with children. They are energetic and need substantial daily exercise, including walks, play, and ideally swimming, or they may become bored and destructive. Labradors tolerate cold and moderate climates well thanks to their dense water-resistant coat, but they overheat in very hot weather and need shade and water. Common health concerns include hip and elbow dysplasia and a strong tendency toward obesity, so their food must be measured carefully.
