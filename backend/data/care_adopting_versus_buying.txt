Prospective owners generally choose between adopting from a shelter or rescue and buying from a breeder, and each path has responsibilities. Adoption gives a home to a dog in need, is usually less expensive, and often provides an adult dog with a known temperament, though history may be incomplete. Buying from a breeder can offer predictability of size, coat, and temperament, but only responsible breeders who health-test their dogs and prioritise welfare should be supported; buying from puchi farms or impulse sources fuels animal suffering. Whichever route, the goal is a healthy, well-matched dog from a source that cares about the animals' welfare.
