The Jack Russell Terrier is a small, extremely energetic working terrier bred for fox hunting. They are bold, clever, and tireless, with an intensity that surprises owners expecting a low-maintenance small dog; they need far more exercise and stimulation than their size suggests. Their strong prey drive and digging instinct require management and secure fencing. Jack Russells are generally healthy and long-lived but can be prone to eye conditions and luxating patellas. They suit active owners rather than those wanting a calm lapdog.
