Reading canine body language helps owners understand and protect their dogs. A relaxed dog has a loose body, soft eyes, and a naturally wagging tail, while stress shows through a tucked tail, flattened ears, lip-licking, yawning, and a tense posture. A common misconception is that any wagging tail means friendliness; a high, stiff wag can signal arousal or threat. Signs a dog wants space include turning away, freezing, or showing the whites of the eyes, and ignoring these can lead to bites. Teaching children to recognise these signals and to give dogs space is an important safety measure.
