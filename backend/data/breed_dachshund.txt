The Dachshund is a small scent hound with a long body and short legs, originally bred to hunt badgers underground. They are bold, lively, and affectionate but famously stubborn, which can make training a patient process. Their long spine makes them highly prone to intervertebral disc disease, so they should be discouraged from jumping on and off furniture and kept at a healthy weight to protect their backs. Dachshunds adapt well to apartments but still need daily walks and can be vocal. Careful weight management is one of the most important things an owner can do for this breed.
