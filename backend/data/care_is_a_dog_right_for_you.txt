Before getting a dog, it is worth honestly assessing whether your life suits one, because a dog is a commitment of ten to fifteen years or more. Consider your daily schedule, since dogs need company, exercise, and toileting breaks and most do not cope well being alone all day. Consider your budget, activity level, living space, travel habits, and whether everyone in the household wants a dog. Different breeds demand very different amounts of time, exercise, and grooming, so matching a dog to your real lifestyle matters more than choosing one you find appealing. Deciding not to get a dog, or to wait, is a responsible choice if the fit is not right.
