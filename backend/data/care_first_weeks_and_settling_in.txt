The first weeks with a new dog are a settling-in period often described by the "rule of three": roughly three days to begin decompressing, three weeks to start learning the routine, and three months to feel truly at home. During this time a dog's real personality gradually emerges as it relaxes, so early behaviour is not always representative. Establish consistent routines for feeding, walks, toilet breaks, and rest, as predictability builds security. Begin gentle training and socialisation, keep introductions to new people and situations positive and gradual, and avoid overwhelming the dog. Giving a new dog time and patience during this period prevents many future problems.
