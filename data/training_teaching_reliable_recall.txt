Reliable recall, coming when called, is the most safety-critical skill a dog can learn, since it can prevent road accidents and dangerous encounters. Teach it using high-value rewards so that returning to you is always the best option, and begin in a quiet, distraction-free space before gradually adding distractions and distance. Never call a dog to you for something unpleasant, and never punish a dog that comes back slowly, as both teach the dog that returning is a bad idea. Use a long training line for safety while building reliability outdoors. Recall is never truly finished and benefits from lifelong practice and reinforcement.
