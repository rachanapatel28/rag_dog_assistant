Rules on leashing and where dogs may go in Germany are set regionally and locally, so they differ from place to place. Many areas require dogs to be leashed in towns, parks, and near roads, while some designated dog areas (Hundewiesen or Hundeauslaufflächen) allow off-leash exercise. Certain seasons and locations, such as nature reserves during breeding periods, may impose stricter leash rules. Dogs are welcome in many German cafés, restaurants, and on public transport, though policies vary and some transport requires a ticket or muzzle for larger dogs. Owners are legally responsible for cleaning up after their dogs. Checking local city rules avoids fines and conflicts.
