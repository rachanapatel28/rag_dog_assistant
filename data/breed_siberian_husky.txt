The Siberian Husky is a medium-sized working dog bred by the Chukchi people to pull sleds across the Arctic. They are friendly, energetic, and independent, but their intelligence and stubbornness make them a challenging choice for first-time owners. Their thick double coat insulates them against extreme cold, which also makes them poorly suited to hot climates such as Spain or southern Italy, where they overheat easily. Huskies have a very high exercise requirement and a strong prey drive, and they are notorious escape artists who will roam if bored or under-exercised. They are generally healthy but can be prone to certain eye conditions.
