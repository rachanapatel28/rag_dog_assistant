The Chihuahua is the smallest dog breed, bold and loyal with a big personality. They bond intensely with their owners and can be wary of strangers, and without socialisation they may become snappy, so gentle early training matters. Their tiny size makes them fragile around small children and very sensitive to cold, so they often need coats in winter. They need only modest exercise and suit apartment life. Common issues include dental disease due to crowded teeth, a soft spot on the skull, and luxating patellas (slipping kneecaps).
