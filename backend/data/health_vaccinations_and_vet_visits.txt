Routine veterinary care and vaccination protect dogs from serious preventable diseases. Core vaccines guard against illnesses such as parvovirus, distemper, and rabies, with the exact schedule and legal requirements varying by region, so owners should follow their local vet's guidance. Puppies receive a series of vaccinations in their first months, followed by periodic boosters. Annual or twice-yearly wellness checks let a vet catch problems early, monitor weight and teeth, and update parasite prevention. Establishing a relationship with a vet before an emergency arises is strongly recommended. Pet insurance or a savings buffer helps manage unexpected veterinary costs.
