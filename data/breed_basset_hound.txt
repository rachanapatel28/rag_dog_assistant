The Basset Hound is a short-legged scent hound with long ears and a laid-back, friendly temperament. They are relatively low-energy and good-natured, but as scent hounds they are stubborn and easily distracted by smells, making training slow. Their long ears and heavy build predispose them to ear infections, obesity, and back and joint strain, so weight control is essential. Bassets tolerate moderate climates but should not be over-exercised. They can be vocal, with a distinctive deep bay, which apartment dwellers should consider.
