Loose-leash walking, where the dog walks without pulling, makes walks enjoyable and is taught through consistency rather than force. The core principle is that pulling never gets the dog where it wants to go: stop or change direction when the leash goes tight, and reward the dog for walking near you with a slack leash. A well-fitted harness gives better control and avoids pressure on the neck, especially for dogs that pull. Progress can be slow because sniffing and exploring are highly rewarding to dogs, so patience and frequent short practice sessions work better than long frustrating walks. Rewarding attention and check-ins builds a habit of staying connected.
