Many dogs experience fear of specific triggers such as thunderstorms, fireworks, or unfamiliar people, and how owners respond matters. Comforting a frightened dog does not reinforce fear, so it is fine to provide reassurance and a safe hiding place. For predictable events like fireworks, creating a den, using background noise, and closing curtains can help, and in severe cases a vet may advise anti-anxiety support. Never force a fearful dog to confront what scares it, as flooding can worsen the phobia. Gradual desensitisation and counter-conditioning, ideally with professional guidance, are the evidence-based ways to reduce fear over time.
