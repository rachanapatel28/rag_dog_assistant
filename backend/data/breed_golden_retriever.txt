The Golden Retriever is a medium-to-large gundog known for its gentle, eager-to-please temperament, making it an excellent choice for first-time owners and families. Goldens are highly social and do not cope well with being left alone for long periods, which can lead to anxiety. They require significant daily exercise and mental stimulation because they were bred to work closely with people. Their thick double coat handles cold well but sheds heavily and makes them prone to overheating in hot climates. The breed is predisposed to hip and elbow dysplasia and to certain cancers, so regular veterinary checks are important.
