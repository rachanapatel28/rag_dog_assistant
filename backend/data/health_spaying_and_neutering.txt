Spaying (females) and neutering (males) are common surgical procedures that prevent reproduction and can bring health and behavioural benefits. They prevent unwanted litters, which helps reduce the number of dogs in shelters, and can reduce certain health risks such as some cancers and infections, as well as some hormone-driven behaviours. The ideal timing depends on the individual dog's breed, size, and health, and there is ongoing veterinary discussion about optimal age, so the decision and timing should be made in consultation with a vet. As with any surgery there are risks and a recovery period. Owners should discuss the pros, cons, and timing for their specific dog with their veterinarian.
