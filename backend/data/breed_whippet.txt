The Whippet is a medium sighthound built for speed, yet surprisingly calm and affectionate indoors, often happy to lounge for much of the day. They need short bursts of hard exercise, ideally safe off-lead sprinting, but their strong prey drive means recall around small animals is unreliable. Their thin coat and low body fat make them very sensitive to cold, so they need coats in winter and soft bedding. Whippets are among the healthier breeds, though their sensitivity to anaesthesia and cold are worth noting. They suit both active and relatively quiet homes.
