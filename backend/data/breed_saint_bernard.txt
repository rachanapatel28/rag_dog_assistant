The Saint Bernard is a giant Alpine breed historically used for mountain rescue, known for a calm, gentle, and patient temperament. Their enormous size means high food and veterinary costs and a need for space, and their thick coat makes them very heat-intolerant. They need moderate, controlled exercise, especially as puppies, to protect their joints. Like most giant breeds they have a short lifespan and are prone to bloat, hip and elbow dysplasia, and heart conditions. They also drool heavily, which prospective owners should expect.
