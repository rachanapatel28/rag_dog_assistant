Having the right supplies ready before bringing a dog home avoids a stressful scramble. Essentials include a well-fitted collar with an ID tag, a leash and harness, food and water bowls, an appropriate diet, a comfortable bed, and a crate if you plan to crate train. Add safe chew toys and enrichment toys, grooming tools suited to the coat type, poo bags, and cleaning supplies with an enzymatic cleaner for accidents. For puppies, easily washable bedding helps. Buying quality basics up front, rather than everything at once, is usually more sensible than over-purchasing before you know the dog's preferences.
