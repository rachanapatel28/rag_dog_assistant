Parasite prevention is a routine but essential part of dog care. External parasites like fleas and ticks cause itching and can transmit diseases, with ticks in particular spreading serious illnesses, so regular preventive treatment is important, especially in warmer months and grassy areas. Internal parasites such as roundworms, tapeworms, and heartworm require routine deworming and, in heartworm regions, specific preventives. The appropriate products and schedule depend on where the dog lives and its lifestyle, so this should be guided by a vet. Checking the coat after walks in tick-prone areas and prompt tick removal reduce disease risk.
