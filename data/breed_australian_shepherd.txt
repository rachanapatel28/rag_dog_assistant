The Australian Shepherd is a medium herding breed, energetic and highly trainable, developed for working livestock on ranches. Aussies are affectionate and eager to please but need a great deal of exercise and mental stimulation, making them a poor fit for low-activity households. They excel at dog sports and farm work. Their coat handles varied climates but requires regular grooming and sheds seasonally. The breed can carry a genetic sensitivity to certain drugs and is prone to some inherited eye conditions, so genetic testing of breeding dogs is important.
