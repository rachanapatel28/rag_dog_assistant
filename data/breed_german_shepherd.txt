The German Shepherd is a large, highly intelligent working breed developed for herding and later widely used in police and military roles. They are confident, loyal, and trainable, but their intelligence and drive mean they need experienced, consistent handling and a great deal of mental and physical work, so they suit committed rather than casual first-time owners. Without enough stimulation they can become anxious or protective in unhelpful ways. Their double coat tolerates cold well and sheds heavily year-round. The breed is well known for a predisposition to hip and elbow dysplasia and degenerative myelopathy, a spinal condition of older dogs.
