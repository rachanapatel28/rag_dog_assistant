Positive reinforcement is the most effective and humane foundation for dog training: you reward the behaviour you want, which makes the dog more likely to repeat it. Rewards can be food, praise, play, or anything the dog values, delivered immediately so the dog links the reward to the right action. This approach builds confidence and a strong bond, whereas punishment-based methods can create fear and aggression and damage trust. Timing, consistency, and keeping sessions short and positive are key. Modern trainers and veterinary behaviourists overwhelmingly recommend reward-based training over dominance or punishment-based techniques.
