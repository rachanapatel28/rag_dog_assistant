The Newfoundland is a giant working breed bred for water rescue and hauling, known for a sweet, patient temperament that makes it wonderful with children. Their thick water-resistant double coat suits cold and water but makes them very heat-intolerant and requires regular grooming. They are strong swimmers and moderate in energy, needing space and controlled exercise to protect developing joints. Newfoundlands drool heavily and, like most giant breeds, have a short lifespan and a predisposition to hip and elbow dysplasia and heart conditions.
