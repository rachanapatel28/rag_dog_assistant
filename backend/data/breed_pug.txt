The Pug is a small companion breed known for its charming, sociable, and even-tempered nature, which makes it popular with families and first-time owners. Pugs are low-energy and well suited to apartments, but as a brachycephalic breed they have significant breathing difficulties and are dangerously intolerant of heat and exertion. They also gain weight easily, which worsens their breathing. Common health problems include eye injuries due to their prominent eyes, skin-fold infections, and spinal issues. Owners must keep them cool, lean, and gently exercised.
