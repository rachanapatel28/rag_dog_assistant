The Rottweiler is a large, powerful working breed originally used to drive cattle and pull carts. They are confident, calm, and loyal, and form strong bonds with their families, but their size and guarding instincts mean they need early socialisation and firm, knowledgeable training, making them better suited to experienced owners. They require regular exercise and mental engagement to stay balanced. Rottweilers tolerate cold reasonably but can overheat. The breed has a shorter average lifespan and is predisposed to hip and elbow dysplasia, certain cancers, and heart conditions, so responsible breeding and veterinary monitoring are important.
