Most owners eventually need care for their dog while away, and planning ahead reduces stress for everyone. Options include a trusted friend or family member, a professional pet sitter who visits or stays, doggy daycare for social dogs, or a boarding kennel. When choosing paid care, visit in advance, ask about supervision, vaccination requirements, and how dogs are grouped, and consider the dog's temperament, since not all dogs enjoy busy daycare settings. Leaving familiar bedding, clear feeding and medication instructions, and emergency contacts helps. Gradually acclimatising a dog to being cared for by others, before it is essential, makes future separations easier.
