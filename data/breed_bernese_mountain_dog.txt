The Bernese Mountain Dog is a large working breed from the Swiss Alps, calm, affectionate, and good-natured with families. Their thick tricolour coat suits cold climates and makes them very heat-intolerant, so they need to be kept cool in summer. They are moderate in energy and enjoy outdoor activity but should not be over-exercised while their joints are developing. Sadly the breed has one of the shorter lifespans among dogs and a high cancer risk, along with a predisposition to hip and elbow dysplasia, so responsible breeding is especially important.
