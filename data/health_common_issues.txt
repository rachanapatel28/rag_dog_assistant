Certain health issues appear across many breeds and are worth every owner understanding. Hip and elbow dysplasia are inherited joint conditions common in larger breeds and cause pain and arthritis. Obesity, largely preventable, worsens nearly every other condition. Dental disease is extremely common and affects overall health. Ear infections are frequent in floppy-eared breeds. Bloat, or gastric torsion, is a life-threatening emergency in deep-chested breeds and requires immediate veterinary care. Understanding a chosen breed's specific predispositions, and buying from health-testing breeders or adopting with a vet check, helps owners prepare and catch problems early.
