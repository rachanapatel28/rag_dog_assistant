Owning a dog is a significant long-term financial commitment that prospective owners often underestimate. Beyond the initial adoption or purchase price, ongoing costs include quality food, routine veterinary care and vaccinations, parasite prevention, grooming, equipment, training, and boarding or daycare. Unexpected veterinary emergencies can cost large sums, which is why pet insurance or a dedicated savings buffer is widely recommended. Larger breeds cost more to feed and treat, and some breeds have predictable high veterinary needs. Realistically budgeting for a dog's entire life, which can be over a decade, is an essential part of responsible ownership.
