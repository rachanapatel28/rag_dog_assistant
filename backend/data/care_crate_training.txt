Crate training, done kindly, gives a dog a safe den and helps with house training and settling. The crate should be sized so the dog can stand, turn, and lie down comfortably, and it should always be associated with positive things like treats, meals, and calm rest, never used as punishment. Introduce it gradually, letting the dog enter voluntarily and building up time slowly so it never feels like a trap. A properly crate-trained dog will often choose to rest there. Crates also make travel and vet visits less stressful, but dogs should not be crated for long stretches during the day.
