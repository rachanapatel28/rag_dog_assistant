Homes and gardens contain hazards beyond toxic foods that owners should manage. Antifreeze is sweet-tasting and lethal even in tiny amounts and must be stored and cleaned up carefully. Many human medications, including common painkillers, are highly toxic to dogs and should never be given or left accessible. Certain plants such as lilies, sago palm, and others can poison dogs, as can rodent poisons, slug pellets, cleaning products, and some essential oils. Small objects, batteries, and cooked bones pose choking or internal-injury risks. If poisoning is suspected, contact a vet or poison line immediately with details; rapid action greatly improves outcomes.
