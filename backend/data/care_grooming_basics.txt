Grooming needs depend heavily on coat type, and neglecting them causes real discomfort and health problems. Double-coated breeds shed seasonally and need regular brushing to remove loose undercoat, while curly or silky coats like those of Poodles and Shih Tzus mat easily and require frequent brushing and professional clipping. Regardless of coat, routine care includes nail trimming to prevent painful overgrowth, ear cleaning (especially in floppy-eared breeds prone to infection), and dental care. Introducing grooming gently and early makes it stress-free for life. Matting, overgrown nails, and dirty ears are common but avoidable sources of pain.
