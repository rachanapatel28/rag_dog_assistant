Travelling within the European Union with a dog is made straightforward by the EU Pet Passport system, which is useful for owners in Germany. The passport is issued by an authorised vet and records the dog's microchip and rabies vaccination, among other details, and a valid rabies vaccination is generally required for travel between EU countries. Some destinations have additional requirements, such as tapeworm treatment for certain countries, so checking the specific rules of the destination before travelling is important. Requirements for travel outside the EU can be considerably more complex and should be researched well in advance. A vet can advise on the current documentation and vaccinations needed for a planned trip.
