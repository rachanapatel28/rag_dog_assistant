The Shih Tzu is a small companion breed developed as a palace lapdog, affectionate and adaptable with a friendly disposition that suits apartments and first-time owners. They need only modest exercise but require frequent grooming, as their long coat mats easily and often needs professional clipping. As a brachycephalic breed they have some heat sensitivity and mild breathing limitations. Common health issues include eye problems due to their prominent eyes, dental crowding, and ear infections, so routine grooming and cleaning are important parts of their care.
