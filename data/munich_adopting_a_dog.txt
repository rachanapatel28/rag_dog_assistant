Adopting a dog in Munich typically goes through an animal shelter (Tierheim) or a rescue organisation, and the process is designed to ensure good matches rather than quick handovers. Prospective adopters usually complete an application, meet the dog, sometimes undergo a home-suitability conversation or visit, and pay an adoption fee that helps cover the animal's care. Bavaria classifies certain breeds as listed or potentially dangerous, which can bring extra keeping requirements, so this is worth checking before adopting. IMPORTANT: exact shelter names, addresses, opening hours, fees, and current requirements change and must be taken from official sources rather than assumed. Always confirm details directly with the shelter and the City of Munich before acting.
