The Cavalier King Charles Spaniel is a small, gentle companion breed that is affectionate, adaptable, and good with children and other pets, making it an excellent choice for first-time and older owners. They are moderate in energy and happy with daily walks and plenty of companionship, but they dislike being left alone. Their silky coat needs regular brushing. The breed's most serious concern is a strong predisposition to mitral valve disease, a heart condition, as well as a neurological condition called syringomyelia, so buying from health-testing breeders is essential.
