A critical safety rule is never to give a dog human medication unless a veterinarian specifically directs it. Common over-the-counter painkillers such as ibuprofen and paracetamol (acetaminophen) are highly toxic to dogs and can cause severe, sometimes fatal, damage even at small doses. Many other human drugs are dangerous or dosed completely differently for dogs. If a dog is in pain, the correct action is to contact a vet, who can prescribe safe, appropriate medication, rather than reaching for the household medicine cabinet. Keep all medications securely stored, as accidental ingestion of dropped pills is a common cause of poisoning. This is general safety information.
