Deciding between a puppy and an adult dog is a major choice with real trade-offs. Puppies require enormous time and patience for house training, socialisation, and managing chewing and sleep disruption, and their adult temperament is not fully predictable. Adult dogs, often available through shelters and rescues, are frequently already house-trained and have a known personality and size, making them a lower-effort and lower-surprise option that suits busy or first-time owners well. Senior dogs can make especially calm, grateful companions. Adopting an adult also directly frees up shelter space, which is why many first-time owners are encouraged to consider one.
