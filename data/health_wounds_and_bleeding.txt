Minor cuts and grazes can often be managed at home, while serious wounds need veterinary care. For minor wounds, gently clean the area and monitor for swelling, discharge, or signs of infection. For heavier bleeding, apply firm, steady pressure with a clean cloth or bandage and seek veterinary help, as significant blood loss is dangerous. Deep wounds, bites, anything with embedded objects, or wounds that will not stop bleeding require prompt professional treatment and possibly antibiotics or stitches. Even calm dogs may snap when hurt, so handle injured areas carefully. When in doubt about the severity of a wound, contacting a vet is the safe choice.
