A dog's first day in a new home is overwhelming for it, so the goal is calm and predictability rather than excitement. Keep arrivals low-key, introduce the dog slowly to one area of the home, and let it explore at its own pace without crowding or too many visitors. Show it where water, food, bed, and the toilet area are, and begin the toileting routine immediately by taking it outside regularly. Expect some anxiety, reluctance to eat, or a quiet, withdrawn dog on day one; this is normal. Patience and a gentle, consistent routine help the dog begin to feel safe.
